# The Way I Think

If you ask me, every problem has an infinite number of solutions.

What I love is searching through them — not just for something that works, but for the **smartest way to make it work.**

Sometimes the smartest solution is elegant.

Sometimes it's ridiculously simple.

Sometimes it requires rebuilding the entire approach.

And sometimes you have to break the first solution before you understand the right one.

That's the part I enjoy.

> **There are infinite ways to solve a problem.  
> I want to find the one worth building.**

# Selected Work

## 🚁 ASCEND

### Autonomous System for Cooperative Exploration, Navigation and Docking

An autonomous drone system developed for the **ISRO Robotics Challenge**.

The project explores autonomous navigation, perception, sensor fusion, and docking in environments where conventional positioning assumptions cannot simply be relied upon.

**Focus**

`Robotics` · `Autonomous Navigation` · `Computer Vision` · `Sensors` · `Embedded Systems`

---

## 🆘 SafeRoute

### Disaster Survival Navigation — Designed for Failure

An emergency-focused Progressive Web App designed around a simple question:

> **What happens when the infrastructure people normally depend on stops working?**

SafeRoute explores:

- Emergency navigation
- Safe-house discovery
- Compass guidance
- Family coordination
- SOS communication
- High-contrast emergency interfaces
- Alternative communication infrastructure

**Stack**

`React` · `Next.js` · `TypeScript` · `Tailwind CSS` · `PWA`

---

## 🏺 THADAM

### Digital Preservation of Ancient and Medical Recipes

An AI-powered system exploring how traditional and medical knowledge can be preserved and transformed into structured digital information.

The system combines:

- Computer Vision
- Object Detection
- Speech Recognition
- AI-based information extraction

**Stack**

`YOLOv8` · `Whisper` · `AI` · `Computer Vision`
